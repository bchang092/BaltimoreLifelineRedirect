ABOUT LUMAREACH

Community first.
Technology with purpose.

Our mission is to put verified community resources within reach.

01 — Our community

It starts with Baltimore.

We run Baltimore Lifeline, a community resource site that helps people find local support. It’s the first chapter of LumaReach—and the reason we started.

Our focus is information people can use: checked against sources, clearly organized, and revisited as things change.

02 — A sustainable mission

The same technology.
A wider purpose.

We aim to sustain our community work by offering the technology behind it as a paid service to businesses and organizations.

From web scraping and AI-assisted verification to custom data pipelines, we help turn scattered information into useful datasets that stay up to date.

  • Collect information
  • Check and organize
  • Keep it current

Useful data for your work. Lasting support for ours.
